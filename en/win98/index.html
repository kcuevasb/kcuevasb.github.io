Chapter 01 — how I got into computers

      
I'm Kepa Cuevas Barrasa, from Bilbao. The story starts here: a desktop like this one, a lot of
      curiosity about what was behind the windows, and the feeling that you could break and fix
      anything without asking anyone for permission.

      
Everything else came out of that — studying development, and ending up doing this for a living.
      But before the first professional line of code there were pizza shifts and a life away from the
      screen that is still very much intact: have a look at the other desktop icons.

      
The next chapters cover the rest: where I have worked, and what I build when nobody asks me to.
