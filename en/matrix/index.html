The other chapters cover where I have worked. This one covers what I do after work: mobile apps,

        not mine — and that is where AI comes in. The interesting part is not that I use it, it is where
        it ends and where I begin.

// 01 · whoami

      

        
This is the self-taught side: what I have built on my own, in my own time. None of these projects
        came from a ticket — all of them from "what if I tried building this?".

        
And here comes the honest part: except for cartelera-app (Java and Spring, my home turf), they are
        written in languages I do not master. What I bring is not typing Kotlin from memory, but knowing
        what I want to build, how it should be structured, and recognising when what comes out is not
        good enough and has to be redone. AI supplies the language; the engineering judgement is mine —
        which is exactly what I have been doing since 2017.

// 03 · self_taught_projects --with-ai

      
Real projects, finished and in use — starting with me. None of them is a demo or a half-finished
        tutorial: if they are here it is because they work. The code is private; what can be shown is what they
        are for and how they were thought out. The ones marked spec-first are built the
        other way round: first the project constitution and the specification of each feature,
        and only once that is settled, the code.


      


        

          
cartelera-app Java · Spring Boot · in production spec-first

          
A personal replacement for TV Time when it shut down: following series and films, with friends, chat and an activity feed. In production, with real users who are not me, and 474 titles migrated without losing one.

          
JavaSpring BootArq. HexagonalPostgreSQLDockerRender

          

            
Three decisions

            
    

            
  • Hexagonal · every new integration goes in without touching what already works. Three times so far: SendGrid, Firebase and the OMDb ratings.
    • 
            
  • Caching · 6h on already-published records; search and trending deliberately left out, because there a stale result is a wrong one.
    • 
            
  • Incremental saving · marking one episode as watched used to rewrite the whole library; normal edits now go item by item.
    •

> in specification... ITVApp · Native Android + Spring Boot

          
A vehicle-inspection app meant to work with no signal, with the phone strapped to your forearm and gloves on. Right now it is 23 specification documents and zero lines of code, on purpose: the project constitution and the contracts come first.

          
Already settled before writing anything: the backend is the source of truth and implicit "last write wins" is forbidden — if a sync discards client data, that is recorded and can be looked up.
