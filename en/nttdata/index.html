5@RestController
6@RequestMapping("/api/nttdata/experience")
7public class ExperienceController {
8
9    private static final Logger LOG =
10            LoggerFactory.getLogger(ExperienceController.class);
11
12    @Autowired
13    private LearningRepository repository;
14
15    @Autowired
16    private WsSisnetClient wsSisnet;   // SOAP, IMQ
17
18    @PostMapping("/projects")
19    @Transactional
20    public List<Learning> work(@RequestBody ClientInput client) {
21
22        LOG.info("New project for {}", client.getClient());
23
24        if (client.usa(Integration.SOAP)) {
25            wsSisnet.validarConexion();
26        }
27
28        // What comes in is a client; what stays is what was learned.
29        return repository.saveAll(Learning.de(client));
30    }
31}

# NTT Data · Jul 2017 – Nov 2022

My first contract as a developer, after a year at Domino's Pizza and six months at Telepizza as shift manager (that one is in chapter 01).

Five years of analysis, design and management of developments, maintenance and enhancements, and a fair few production incidents. This is where I learned what no course teaches: how to defend a change in front of the client, and that the code you write today will be maintained by someone else three years from now.

The following stages (Knowmad Mood and Sopra Steria) have their own chapter.
